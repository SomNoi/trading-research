## Verdict
No edge. Every type, side, SL variant, year, split half and Thai session loses money after cost. Expectancy is about -$0.24 to -$0.50 per trade, against a $0.28 cost, so the gross edge before cost is roughly zero to slightly negative (about -$0.04/trade on ALL). The signal is essentially a coin flip, and the cost decides the result. Win rate stays 3-8 pts below breakeven in every cell. Buys lose less than sells (the 2019-25 gold uptrend), but buys are negative too. The 2025 / last-30% improvement for buys follows the trend; it is not a sign of an edge. "Win" = any trade closing with P&L > 0 after cost, so time exits are included. Max hold is 24 bars, so most fixed-SL trades end on the time stop (42-65%), not at TP or SL.
