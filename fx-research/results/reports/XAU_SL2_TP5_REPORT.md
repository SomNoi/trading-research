# XAUUSD M5 close-break — SL $2 / TP $5 report

Data: Dukascopy XAU/USD mid M5, 2019-01-01 → 2025-10-09, 2107 days. Entry = signal close; SL $2, TP $5; same-bar both = SL; max hold 48 bars then exit at close; cost $0.28/oz; $ at 0.01 lot. Overlapping trades allowed. **Breakeven win rate = (2+0.28)/(5+2) = 32.6%** for SL/TP exits only (time exits blur it). Win = P&L>0 after cost. T2 ⊂ T1; ALL = distinct signals. Script: scripts/xau_sl2_tp5.py

Filter (my mechanical reading of the manual method; the original is discretionary, so treat these numbers as one approximation): (i) H1 bias: fractal swings (3 bars each side, used only after confirmation). Bias = +1 after a completed H1 bar closes above the last confirmed swing high (BOS), −1 after a close below the last confirmed swing low, held until the opposite break. (ii) M15 impulse: last confirmed M15 swing low (2/2 fractal) → highest M5 high since then = leg (must be ≥ $3). (iii) Pullback: deepest low since the leg high retraced 40–60% of the leg (not more), and price is still below the leg high. (iv) M5 close-break BUY signal at that point. SELL mirrored. "Prior support" is approximated by the 40–60% zone (no separate S/R levels).
