**Data labels:**
- **Source:** Dukascopy XAU/USD (our file XAUUSD), M1 bid+ask → mid → M5/H1, UTC (hours in ICT = UTC+7 where stated). Historical 2019-01-01..2025-10-09; the holdout was not loaded.
- **Results:** BACKTEST with Kai's IC Markets costs (XAU p90 spread 11 points London / 10 points NY in these windows; $3.50/lot/side; $0.05/oz/side slippage; swap with Wednesday ×3). Flat 1% risk ($100 per R on $10k). IS/OOS is labelled per table.

**Pre-registration:** prereg/XAU_SOX_PREREG.md (sha256 5ff6f1bb…, 2026-10-15T06:41:24Z) and the amendment prereg/XAU_SOX_V2_PREREG.md (sha256 41fce6b4…, 06:43:13Z), each written before its run.
- **Fixed to stay in budget:**
  - both windows traded in every variant (London vs NY reported, not selected);
  - a single SL rule: the expansion bar's opposite end + $0.10, $3 floor;
  - quiet threshold 0.7;
  - expansion 1.5 × ATR14 with the close in the top/bottom 25%;
  - first qualifying bar per window only.
- **Grid (20 per version):** entry (close | stop-next-bar) × H1 EMA55 direction (none | with) × exit:
  - A = 1R;
  - B = 1.5R;
  - C = 50% at 1R + trail under M5 3-3 swings;
  - D = 6-bar time stop;
  - E = quick scalp: TP = net +$1/oz after estimated cost, SL to breakeven after +$0.60/oz.
- **Windows:** fixed UTC (London 07:00–09:00, NY 12:30–14:30), no DST shift.
