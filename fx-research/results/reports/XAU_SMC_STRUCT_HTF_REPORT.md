## Verdict
- **Not tradeable.** The higher timeframes shrink the cost to about 0.04R (H1) and 0.02R (H4), so costs stop being the problem. But **gross** expectancy is still about 0R (H1 +0.003 to +0.034R, H4 −0.18 to +0.08R at 1.5R).

- **Session split:** H1 London (14–19 Thai) and NY-late (00–07 Thai) are positive in both halves; Asia and NY 19–24 are negative. With 25–176 trades per cell, this is a candidate for a pre-registered test, not evidence.
