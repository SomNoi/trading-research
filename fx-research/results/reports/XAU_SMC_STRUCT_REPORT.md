# XAUUSD M5 — fuzzy structural SMC continuation (research only)
Script: `scripts/xau_smc_struct.py`. Data M5 from XAUUSD_M1 2019-01-02 → 2025-10-09 (holdout ≥2025-10-10 untouched).
IS = before 2023-10-04 (1479 days), OS = 2023-10-04 → 2025-10-09 (628 days). Cost $0.28/oz RT; 0.01 lot = 1 oz so USD = R × risk$.
No martingale; one trade per setup; entry next M5 open after confirmation close; SL = pullback/sweep extreme ± 0.1 ATR; max hold 288 bars (24h) then close; entries blocked ≥20:30 UTC and weekends.

## Method
- Zigzag swings, reversal threshold k×ATR14, k ∈ {2, 3, 4.5}; a pivot is usable only after its confirmation bar (no lookahead).
- Bullish: swing high H1 > previous swing high (BOS/HH), impulse L0→H1. Pullback tracked live; invalid if low < L0 or new high > H1; must be 30–70% retrace at confirmation (buckets 38.2/50/61.8 ±5%).
- Sweep = pullback's new low undercut an earlier pullback minor low and the bar closed back above it. OB = last opposite candle before L0; touched if pullback reached it.
- Confirmation: bullish engulfing, impulse bar (body ≥0.8 ATR), or ChoCh (close above high of the 6 bars before the pullback low). Mirror for bearish.
- Feature vector: [retrace, log(impulse bars / pullback bars), wick/body of conf bar, impulse/ATR, sweep], z-scored on IS. Similarity: cosine, and exp(-‖z−T‖/√5) ("dist"); accept ≥0.70. Templates: hand-made ideal [0.55, impulse 2× faster, 0.5, 3 ATR, sweep] and KMeans(3) centroids of IS 1.5R winners (max over centroids).
- TPs: 1R, 1.5R, 2R, prior impulse extreme (IMP). Breakeven win = avgLoss/(avgWin+avgLoss) in net R.
