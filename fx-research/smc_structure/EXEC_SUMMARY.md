# SMC STRUCTURE — executive summary (A–C only)
**NOT a tradable edge. Research vocabulary only. 2026-10-27 ICT.**

## A) Structure map
HTF bias (HH/HL or LH/LL) → mark untaken liquidity (equal highs/lows, BSL above / SSL below) → **sweep** (often wick through, close back) → **against-trend break** (retail **CHoCH**, or ICT **MSS** = stop-run first + displacement) → origin **order block** (last opposing candle) and/or **FVG** (3-candle gap; watch **CE** = 50%) → location in **discount** (buys) or **premium** (sells); optional **OTE** 62–79% of the impulse → **with-trend BOS** toward next external liquidity.

LTF refines; HTF subordinates. Failed OB that price later retests from the other side = **breaker**.

## B) Definitions + what kills them (one line each)
- **Swing:** 3-candle fractal; confirmed on the *next* candle’s close — invalid if marked live.
- **HH/HL vs LH/LL:** trend sequence — broken when the last protecting swing (HL or LH) is *closed* through.
- **Internal vs external:** inside the dealing range vs the range extremes / opposing pool.
- **BOS:** close beyond a confirmed swing *with* the trend (continuation).
- **CHoCH:** close beyond the protecting swing *against* the trend (early reversal warning only).
- **ICT MSS:** against-trend short-term break *after* a stop run, with displacement — not identical to every CHoCH.
- **Weak break:** wick only / no displacement / unconfirmed swing.
- **BSL/SSL, EQH/EQL, inducement, sweep:** inferred stop pools and grabs — not a visible order book; sweep ≡ breakout until later closes.
- **OB:** last opposing candle before displacement; **mitigated** when spent/closed through; **breaker** = failed OB, polarity flips.
- **FVG:** gap between candle-1 and candle-3 wicks; **CE** = 50%; fill is optional, not a guarantee.
- **Premium / discount / equilibrium:** above / below / at 50% of a *well-anchored* dealing range.
- **OTE:** 62–79% retrace used *with* structure, not alone; body beyond range origin invalidates.
- **Displacement:** energetic one-sided move (usually leaves FVG); lethargic grind does not count.
- **MTF:** D1/H4 bias, H1 range, M15/M5 refine — HTF wins conflicts on direction.

## C) Honest limits (mislabels / overfitting)
Retail packs over-label every candle as OB/FVG; confuse wick sweeps with BOS/CHoCH; treat CHoCH as a guaranteed reversal; invent precision at 70.5%/CE without evidence; ignore that liquidity is inferred. **This desk’s locked CHoCH test** (XAU M5 Dukascopy, 2026-10-05 notes): ~breakeven to negative after costs, first 70% losing — **failed candidate, not an edge.** Archive Kaze/v5 SMC-flavoured systems were alerts or bogus backtests — not validation.
